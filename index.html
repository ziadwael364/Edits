About

I'm Ziad Wael.

I'm a professional video editor focused on turning raw footage into content that captures attention and keeps viewers watching. My approach goes beyond simply cutting clips together. I focus on pacing, storytelling, retention, sound design, visual flow, and strategic use of motion graphics to make every second of a video purposeful. Whether it's short-form content for Instagram and TikTok or long-form YouTube content, I adapt the editing style to the creator, audience, and goal of the content. I care about the details that most viewers never consciously notice — the timing of a cut, when a visual appears, how the story progresses, and how to prevent the video from feeling slow or repetitive. The goal is simple: create videos that look professional, feel natural, and keep people watching.
